# Attention Allocation Readout — source and receiving law

This repository is the original **Lenticule Science attention-allocation readout provider**. It implements a read-only, conditional computational service. It is not the original mathematical theory, Office/Work allocator, authorized provider chooser, ActivityPub transport, or ledger of Work acceptance.

## Source owners and limits

- The original mathematical source is the separately owned, **private** `lenticule-science/obsmetrologia` project, particularly `Obsmetrologia/Cohomology/AttentionAllocation.lean` and `AllocationSolver.lean`. Its raw Lean text MUST NOT be bundled into this publicly visible package merely to satisfy portable CI.
- The legacy scalar solver's exact exhaustive arithmetic is a separately implemented program; even source-digest attestation does not prove formal extraction or independent Lean kernel receiving. A mathematical theorem receipt requires a *trusted original host selection* with original Lean files whose bytes match both pinned SHA-256 digests **and** a theorem-eligible request. Without that trusted mount, scalar numeric results MAY be offered explicitly **unattested**, but MUST NOT issue a theorem receipt.
- A host intending to attest private source supplies its own trusted `OBSMETROLOGIA_SOURCE_ROOT` pointed at the original owner-selected checkout. Never accept a client-supplied source path or arbitrary remote fetch as attestation. Failure, unavailable source and source drift MUST remain distinguishable.
- The new heterogeneous attention frontier and finite-difference gauges are conditional projections of source-declared costs and receiver-specific proposed gains. They do not derive Work authority, psychological measurements, physical joules, currency, WIT/ACK/ATT or generalized shadow prices.
- Original Work/Actor/Office, candidate rights and provider grants are controlled by Organizing/FeltOS/MetroPlat and the installed receiving Office; this source **cannot** allocate compute by itself.
- Service requests may contain private claims; original privacy, disclosure and independent admission policies apply before sending them through this provider's journal.
